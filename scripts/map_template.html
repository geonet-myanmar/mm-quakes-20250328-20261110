About

Earthquakes: USGS FDSN Event Web Service, events whose epicentre falls inside the Natural Earth 10m Myanmar boundary (plus the offshore Andaman Sea / Bay of Bengal events USGS itself catalogues against Myanmar).

Faults: GEM Global Active Faults Database (Styron & Pagani, 2020), clipped to a 1.5° buffer around Myanmar.

Datasets: data/myanmar_earthquakes.geojson, data/myanmar_active_faults.geojson.
